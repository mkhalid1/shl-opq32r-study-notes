# Glossary

Terms you will meet in SHL's material, your invitation and these notes. The 32 scale names themselves are explained in the three area guides.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Block** | One question in the OPQ32r: a set of three statements you choose between. SHL also calls them triplets. |
| **Comparison group** | The group of earlier respondents your answers are compared with. Also called a norm group. |
| **Competency** | A set of behaviours an employer links to good performance in a role. SHL says the 32 OPQ characteristics can be mapped to job competencies. |
| **Consistency scale** | A scale on SHL's sample OPQ Profile that shows whether someone responded more or less consistently across the questionnaire. |
| **Domain, area** | One of the broad groups of scales: Relationships with People, Thinking Style, and Feelings and Emotions. SHL also mentions Dynamism as a possible fourth. |
| **Forced choice** | A format where you choose between statements instead of rating each one. The OPQ32r uses it. |
| **Normative format** | A format where you rate each statement on its own, for example on a five-point scale. The OPQ32n uses it. |
| **OPQ** | Occupational Personality Questionnaire, published by SHL. |
| **OPQ32r** | The forced-choice version of the OPQ, with 104 questions. SHL calls it the latest and most comprehensive version. |
| **OPQ32n** | The normative version of the OPQ, with 230 questions rated on a five-point scale. |
| **Profile** | The set of scores across all 32 scales, usually shown as a chart. |
| **Reference point** | Our term for the context you answer about, such as your current job. Keeping one reference point helps you answer consistently. |
| **Self-report** | A questionnaire where you describe yourself. SHL's sample candidate report stresses that the OPQ is your own view of your behaviour. |
| **Sten** | A standard score on a 1 to 10 scale. SHL's sample OPQ Profile shows each scale as a sten. |
| **Sub-group** | A group of related scales within an area, such as Influence or Structure. |
| **Unproctored** | Taken without a supervisor. SHL designed the OPQ for unproctored use. |
