# Sources

Every fact in these notes about the questionnaire comes from SHL's own pages and documents. Each source below was checked live on 24 November 2026. If something has changed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mkhalid1/shl-opq32r-study-notes/issues).

## What SHL does not publish

We looked and did not find these on SHL's public pages, so the notes do not state them as fact:

- The scoring model that turns choices into stens
- How the consistency scale is calculated or used
- Any "ideal" or target profile, or a pass mark

## What is our own guidance

The plain-language scale descriptions, the methods, the traps, the study plans and all 15 example blocks and 6 diagnostic statements are our own work. The descriptions summarise each scale in our words. They are not SHL's wording.
