# 15-minute diagnostic

A personality questionnaire has no right answers, so this diagnostic does not test your personality. It tests two things that do help: whether you can recognise what each statement is about, and whether you can answer a block quickly and honestly. Give yourself 15 minutes.

The statements are original, written for these notes, and not taken from the OPQ32r.

## Part A: Relationships with People

For each statement, name the scale it points to. Choose from: Persuasive, Controlling, Outspoken, Independent Minded, Outgoing, Affiliative, Socially Confident, Modest, Democratic, Caring.

**A1.** "I like to be the person who sets the direction for the team."

**A2.** "I talk to everyone involved before I decide."

**A3.** "I am comfortable introducing myself to senior people I have not met."

## Part B: Thinking Style

Choose from: Data Rational, Evaluative, Behavioural, Conventional, Conceptual, Innovative, Variety Seeking, Adaptable, Forward Thinking, Detail Conscious, Conscientious, Rule Following.

**B1.** "I read the small print before I sign anything."

**B2.** "I prefer a method that has worked before to one that has not been tried."

**B3.** "I am always suggesting new ways to do things."

## Part C: Feelings and Emotions

Choose from: Relaxed, Worrying, Tough-minded, Optimistic, Trusting, Emotionally Controlled, Vigorous, Competitive, Achieving, Decisive.

**C1.** "I feel uneasy for days before a big interview."

**C2.** "I set myself goals that stretch me."

**C3.** "I do not let other people see when I am frustrated."

## Part D: One honest block

Answer this block for yourself in under 30 seconds. There is no answer key.

| | Statement |
|---|---|
| A | I check my work for small mistakes. |
| B | I feel at ease meeting people for the first time. |
| C | I make decisions quickly. |

Choose the one most like you at work, then the more like you of the remaining two. Then write one real example, from the last month, for your first choice.

## Answers

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

**A1: Controlling.** Setting the direction and leading the group. *Not Persuasive:* that is about changing views, not taking charge.

**A2: Democratic.** Consulting before deciding. *Not Affiliative:* that is about liking company, not about how you decide.

**A3: Socially Confident.** Ease with new people, including formal or senior ones. *Not Outgoing:* that is about liveliness in groups.

**B1: Detail Conscious.** Care over fine detail. *Not Rule Following:* reading the small print is about accuracy, not obeying procedures.

**B2: Conventional.** Preferring proven methods. *Not Rule Following:* that is about rules and policy, not choice of method.

**B3: Innovative.** Generating new ideas. *Not Variety Seeking:* that is about wanting change in your own routine.

**C1: Worrying.** Nerves before important events. *Not Relaxed:* Relaxed is about your general day-to-day state. Worrying is about big occasions.

**C2: Achieving.** Ambition and demanding goals. *Not Competitive:* the statement is about your own goals, not beating others.

**C3: Emotionally Controlled.** Not showing feelings. *Not Tough-minded:* that is about whether criticism hurts, not whether you show it.

**Part D:** the statements point to Detail Conscious, Socially Confident and Decisive. Your ranking is yours. What matters is whether you could give a real example for your first choice in a few seconds. If you could, you answered from behaviour. If you struggled, you may have answered from how you would like to be.

</details>
