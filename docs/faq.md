# FAQ

The first six questions are the ones Google showed under "People also ask" for UK searches on "shl opq32r personality test" in November 2026. The rest are common questions from candidates. Answers use SHL's own pages where SHL has published something, and say so when it has not.

## What is the SHL personality questionnaire?

The Occupational Personality Questionnaire, or OPQ. SHL describes personality questionnaires as looking at how you like to work, not at your abilities. The OPQ32r version measures 32 characteristics through forced-choice questions. See the [test overview](test-overview.md).

## What are the top 3 personality tests?

There is no official ranking. In hiring you are likely to meet questionnaires built around the Big Five personality factors, the SHL OPQ, and others from different publishers. If your invitation names the OPQ or OPQ32r, it is the one in this repo.

## How to answer the SHL personality test?

Choose the statement most like you at work, then the more like you of the remaining two, as SHL's example page describes. Answer from real behaviour on a normal week, and keep one reference point throughout. See [answering honestly](answering-honestly.md).

## How to pass the OPQ test?

There is no pass mark. SHL describes personality questionnaires as having no right or wrong answers. An employer compares your profile with what a role needs. The best preparation is to understand the format and the scales, answer honestly, and be ready to discuss your profile at interview.

## How does SHL detect cheating?

SHL says the OPQ's forced-choice format, with three equally desirable statements, is designed to reduce faking. Its sample OPQ Profile also shows a Consistency scale. SHL does not publish further detail, so treat any claim about exact checks with caution.

## Is it possible to fail a personality test?

Not in the sense of a mark. An employer may decide a profile does not fit a particular role. That is a judgement about fit, and a role that does not fit how you work is rarely one you would enjoy.

## How long does the OPQ32r take?

It is untimed. SHL gives an average of 15 to 25 minutes on its 2022 fact sheet and about 20 minutes on its product page.

## How many questions are there?

SHL lists 104 questions. Its example page shows each as a set of three statements.

## Is it timed?

No. SHL's fact sheet lists the allowed time as untimed. Still, aim to answer steadily rather than dwell on each block.

## Can I take it on my phone?

SHL says the OPQ was designed mobile-first. Use whatever device lets you concentrate.

## What is the difference between OPQ32r and OPQ32n?

The OPQ32r uses forced choice and has 104 questions. The OPQ32n uses a five-point rating scale and has 230. SHL's OPQ32n fact sheet recommends the OPQ32r for selection and high-stakes use.

## Will I see my results?

That depends on the employer. SHL offers a candidate report among its report range, but the employer decides whether you get feedback.

## Can I retake it?

That is up to the employer. SHL's sample candidate report says a profile is likely to stay a good description of your behaviour at work for about 18 to 24 months, so a recent result may be reused.

## Where can I practise for free?

SHL hosts [example questions](https://www.shl.com/shldirect/en/example-questions/personality-questionnaire/). This repo has 15 example blocks across the three area guides. The free [SHL OPQ32 practice page on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/shl-opq32?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=shl-opq32r) lets you try forced-choice blocks, see a sample result and take a free 55-item trait profiler.
